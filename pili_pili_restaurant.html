Our Story

A Culinary Celebration of Flame & Passion

Nestled along the vibrant KG 28 St in Kimihurura, Kigali, Pili Pili is more than just a restaurant—it's an experience. We combine rustic East African outdoor barbecue traditions with refined international grill techniques.

From our signature peri-peri spiced chicken marinated for 24 hours to our wood-fired gourmet pizzas and ice-cold craft cocktails, every dish is prepared over open charcoal embers to ensure maximum flavor and aroma.
